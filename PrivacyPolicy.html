Data Deletion

You can delete your account from inside the application. Press the "Delete Account" button inside the settings menu and enter the confirmation code. This will delete the following data:

Leaderboard entries cannot be manually deleted, but they will be set to 0 and hidden from the game after account deletion. Some platform-level records, if retained by Unity or other service providers for security or legal purposes, may remain subject to those providers' policies.
